GLUTENGUIDE · CATEGORY GUIDE

What do these labels mean?

The labels describe the type of information available — they are not a guarantee that a meal is safe for someone with celiac disease.

Documented review

Evidence reviewed against published criteria. In a future version, this will mean our team has reviewed specific documents or procedures and recorded what was checked and when. It is not a government certification, an inspection, or a promise of zero risk.

Restaurant-reported

The restaurant says it offers gluten-free options or follows certain procedures. This is a statement, not an independent review. Ask about ingredients, shared equipment, preparation surfaces, and cross-contact.

Public information found · unverified

We found public information, but have not confirmed it directly with the restaurant. This is a provisional research label, not a restaurant statement and not an audit.

Limited information

We do not have enough current, reliable information. This does not mean the restaurant is unsafe or safe. Contact the restaurant directly and do not infer suitability from the label.

About this early demo: Restaurant details were gathered from preliminary public information and have not been independently verified by GlutenGuide. Some listings may not yet fit these categories precisely. Please contact the restaurant directly before making food decisions.
