# Product vision

## Problem

People who need to avoid gluten can struggle to identify restaurants with useful, current information about menu ingredients and cross-contact prevention practices.

## Initial audience and geography

- Initial focus: people with celiac disease and others who need to avoid gluten.
- Initial pilot market: Chicago, Illinois.
- Initial experience: mobile-first web app.

## MVP features

1. Discover restaurants on a map and in a list.
2. Search by venue name or neighborhood.
3. Filter by the status of available information.
4. Read a restaurant profile with sources, review dates and questions to ask.
5. Submit community experiences, subject to moderation.
6. Report outdated or incorrect information.

## Out of scope for the first MVP

- Medical diagnosis or symptom interpretation.
- A promise that a meal is risk-free.
- Automated claims that a restaurant is safe.
- Paid placement affecting safety status.
- Multiple dietary restrictions before the gluten workflow is validated.
- Public user accounts until moderation, privacy and data handling are designed.

## Product principles

- Separate restaurant statements, reviewed evidence and community reports.
- Show who supplied information and when it was last checked.
- Avoid unsupported claims such as “100% safe.”
- A commercial payment can never purchase a higher safety status.
- Make uncertainty visible, not hidden.
- Provide a correction/reporting process.
