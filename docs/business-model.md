# Business model hypotheses

These are hypotheses to validate, not confirmed revenue projections.

## User side

Keep core discovery and safety-related information free during the pilot. User trust is the product's foundation.

## Possible business revenue

- Restaurant subscription for profile management, menu updates, responding to reviews and aggregated usage analytics.
- A clearly scoped paid information-review service, only after criteria, qualifications, conflict-of-interest safeguards and liability are established.
- Optional restaurant tools that do not affect a venue's public information status.

## Independence rules

- Payment must never buy a more favorable safety status.
- Sponsored placement must be clearly labelled and must not be confused with verification.
- Negative or disputed reports must not be suppressed in exchange for payment.
- Any review service should publish its scope, criteria, reviewer role and limitations.

## Pilot questions

- Do users repeatedly open the app when choosing where to eat?
- Which information affects their decision?
- Are restaurants willing to keep menu and procedure information current?
- What evidence can venues reliably provide?
- Would restaurants pay for management tools independent of any status?
- Can the service cover moderation, review and hosting costs at a viable price?
